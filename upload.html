Competition Rules & Regulations
general Guidelines

1. Each participant can participate in 3 categories and must upload 3 photographs per category.

2. All entries must be original photographs taken entirely by the participant.

3. Use of AI/Manipulation is strictly prohibited and will be Disqualified.

4. Ensure all photography work submitted is completely genuine and captured within the requested timeline parameters.

Technical Requirements

1. Maximum images size should be (Under 5MB per upload). You can use your preferred resolution.

2. Only basic global adjustments (Contrast, exposure, saturation) are permitted. Heavy digital composite manipulations are not allowed.

3. You can mask the photo but cannot entirely manipulate the Image. Color correction is allowed

4. AI Noise reduction is not allowed.
