The 800th Esala Maha Perahera

The historic Sabaragamuwa Maha Saman Dewalaya celebrated its monumental 800th annual Esala Maha Perahera festival, marking eight centuries of profound religious heritage, cultural pride, and devotion to Deity Saman—the guardian protector of Sri Pada.

Originating out of deep-rooted rituals initiated centuries ago, this festival brings alive majestic features unique to the Sabaragamuwa province. The grand celebration honors ancient customs through a spectacular progression of cultural phases: the initial spiritual protection through an all-night Parithrana Dhamma Sermon, the symbolic planting of the Kap, followed by the five nights of the internal Maluwe Perahera, the five vibrant nights of the Devele (Veedi) Perahera, and culminating in the magnificent Randoli Maha Perahera pageants.

The milestone procession showcased the grandeur of a bygone era, featuring over 45 diverse traditional dance troupes reflecting Up-Country, Low-Country, and authentic Sabaragamuwa dance heritages, accompanied by the footsteps of over 30 majestic decorated tuskers parading through Ratnapura.
